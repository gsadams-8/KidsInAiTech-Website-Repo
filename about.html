Our Mission

Kids in AI Tech was created to make artificial intelligence accessible, engaging, and fun for students of all backgrounds.

Our goal is to foster curiosity and critical thinking, equipping young minds with the technical skills and ethical understanding needed to navigate the future of technology.

Who We Are

We are a community of educators, developers, and tech enthusiasts dedicated to empowering the next generation of digital creators and innovators.
